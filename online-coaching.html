Online Calisthenics Coaching

Get a custom workout plan, 1-on-1 video feedback, and direct coach access to level up your strength anywhere.

Want to train with the best calisthenics coaches in India but live in a different city? Or perhaps you prefer training at home or in your local park but need professional guidance to unlock skills like the handstand, muscle-up, or planche? Our **Online Calisthenics Coaching Program** brings elite bodyweight training straight to your smartphone.

Why Online Calisthenics Coaching?

Without structured guidance, bodyweight training can quickly lead to plateaus or shoulder/elbow injuries. Online coaching provides the structured accountability and professional eyes you need to make rapid, safe progress:

  • No Location Barriers: Train at home, in a hotel, in a local park, or a commercial gym, anywhere in the world.
  • Customized to Your Equipment: Whether you have a full pull-up bar setup, gymnastics rings, parallel bars, or absolutely zero equipment, your program is tailored to what you have.
  • Precision Form Reviews: Avoid injuries and master clean execution by getting detailed video reviews from your coach.

What is Included in the Online Coaching Program?

This is not a generic PDF workout plan. It is a dynamic, fully interactive, and supportive training partnership:

1. Fully Customized Workout Calendars

We build a weekly routine specifically for your goals (e.g. fat loss, general conditioning, or mastering skills like handstands or front levers). We adjust the sets, reps, and exercises as you progress.

2. Video Form Analysis & Technique Corrections

Form is everything. Record your work sets and upload them. Your coach reviews your videos, points out alignment issues, and gives voice or text feedback to help you refine your technique.

3. Regular Check-ins & Daily Support

Get direct access to your coach via chat. Ask questions about workouts, report recovery levels, and stay motivated with weekly check-in reviews.

4. Nutrition & Habit Coaching

Fuel your body for performance. We provide caloric guidelines, protein targets, and habit tracking to help you optimize your recovery, build muscle, and shed body fat.

How Online Coaching Works (Step-by-Step)

  1. Initial Consultation: You fill out a questionnaire detailing your fitness background, past injuries, equipment access, and specific goals.
  2. Mobility Assessment: You record simple movement patterns (squats, shoulder extensions, etc.) so your coach can assess your starting point.
  3. Plan Creation: We deliver your custom workout program on our training calendar.
  4. Train & Log: You record your workouts, note weights/reps, and upload form videos of your main lifts.
  5. Weekly Review: Your coach reviews your logs, checks form videos, and updates your training plan for the following week.

Online Calisthenics Coaching FAQs

Do I need to be able to do pull-ups to start online coaching?

Not at all. We train clients who are starting from zero pull-ups and zero push-ups. We design your program with progressions that fit your current level of strength.

How do we send training videos?

We use a dedicated coaching platform where you can easily upload videos directly, or you can send them via Google Drive/WhatsApp for quick reviews.

Can I switch from online to in-gym training?

Yes, if you live in Mumbai/Mira Road, you can easily migrate to our in-person training program or mix both with a hybrid membership.
