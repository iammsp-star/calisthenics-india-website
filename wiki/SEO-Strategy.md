## 📊 JSON-LD Schema Structures

We use embedded `application/ld+json` across our landing pages to explicitly communicate our entities to search engines.

### 1. GymOrFitnessCenter Schema (Local Business)
Embedded on the homepage (`index.html`) to signal geographical prominence:
- **Business Details**: Name ("Master Calisthenics India"), Primary image, Price range.
- **Location**: Postal Address linked to `Mira Road East, Mira Bhayandar, Mumbai, Maharashtra`, with precise geo-coordinates (`19.2812`, `72.8561`).
- **Offers**: Integrated directly to show our pricing structure (Group Batch, PT, Kids Fitness).

### 2. FAQPage Schema
Deployed on all informational and local pages to win "People Also Ask" rich snippets on the Google SERP.

### 3. Topic-Specific Schemas
- **Article Schema**: Deployed on `calisthenics-training-india.html` to establish authoritativeness.
- **HowTo Schema**: Deployed on `calisthenics-for-beginners.html` to map out workout progressions.
- **Service Schema**: Deployed on `online-coaching.html` to structure our remote coaching packages.
