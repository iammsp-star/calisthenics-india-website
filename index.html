What is Calisthenics?

Calisthenics is a system of physical training utilizing bodyweight resistance to develop muscular hypertrophy, relative force production, and advanced motor control. Popularized globally, this modality uses structural compound movements, isometric progressions, and joint-mobility conditioning rather than isolated weightlifting machinery.

Do I need experience to join Master Calisthenics India?

No prior athletic background is required. Master Calisthenics India accommodates all fitness levels using customized linear regressions. Beginners start with light incline push-ups and band-assisted rows, building up to full standard floor movements and eventual advanced street workout gymnastics.
